المُضَارِعُ مِنَ المَزِيد
From Past to Present — Forms II to X, and the 4-Letter Verbs

You know the augmented patterns in the past tense. Here is each one in the present: a few simple steps turn اسْتَغْفَرَ into يَسْتَغْفِرُ, with all fourteen pronoun forms and real verses from the Qur’an.

ثلاثي مزيد — II–X رباعي مزيد
Build it

مِنَ المَاضِي إِلَى المُضَارِعFive steps, same for every pattern

1
Drop the opening hamza / alif if the past starts with one (Form IV’s أَ, and the hamzat waṣl ا of VII–X).
2
Add the prefix in front: يَـ تَـ أَـ نَـ.
3
Prefix vowel: fatḥa normally — but ḍamma when the past is a four-letter verb (فَعَّلَ، فَاعَلَ، أَفْعَلَ، فَعْلَلَ).
4
Vowel before the last letter: kasra — except Forms V and VI (and تَفَعْلَلَ), which keep fatḥa.
5
Add the ending for the pronoun (ـُ، ـَانِ، ـُونَ، ـِينَ، ـْنَ).

Forms II · III · IV

The ḍamma-prefix group: يُفَعِّلُ · يُفَاعِلُ · يُفْعِلُ

Why ḍamma?Their pasts are four letters long (فَعَّلَ، فَاعَلَ، أَفْعَلَ), so the prefix takes a ḍamma; the vowel before the last letter is kasra.
يُـ prefixلِّ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
From the Qur’an
وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ
Form II, he + كُمْ — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:151: “and teaches you the Book and wisdom.”
وَيُنَزِّلُ الْغَيْثَ وَيَعْلَمُ مَا فِي الْأَرْحَامِ
Form II, he — Sūrah Luqmān, 31:34: “and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs.”
يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّهِمْ
Form II, they (m.) — Sūrah Ghāfir, 40:7: “they glorify their Lord with praise.”
فَإِنَّهُمْ لَا يُكَذِّبُونَكَ
Form II, they (m.) + كَ — Sūrah al-An‘ām, 6:33: “they do not call you a liar.”
وَمَن جَاهَدَ فَإِنَّمَا يُجَاهِدُ لِنَفْسِهِ
Form III, he — Sūrah al-‘Ankabūt, 29:6: “whoever strives, strives only for himself.”
وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَن يُجَادِلُ فِي اللَّهِ بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ
Form III, he — Sūrah al-Ḥajj, 22:3: “among people is one who disputes about Allah without knowledge.”
وَلَا يَزَالُونَ يُقَاتِلُونَكُمْ
Form III, they (m.) + كُمْ — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:217: “and they will not cease fighting you.”
يُخْرِجُ الْحَيَّ مِنَ الْمَيِّتِ وَيُخْرِجُ الْمَيِّتَ مِنَ الْحَيِّ
Form IV, he — Sūrah al-An‘ām, 6:95: “He brings the living out of the dead, and the dead out of the living.”
وَهُوَ الَّذِي يُرْسِلُ الرِّيَاحَ بُشْرًا
Form IV, he — Sūrah al-A‘rāf, 7:57: “It is He who sends the winds as glad tidings.”
يُدْخِلُ مَن يَشَاءُ فِي رَحْمَتِهِ
Form IV, he — Sūrah al-Insān, 76:31: “He admits whom He wills into His mercy.”
Forms V · VI

The تَـ group: يَتَفَعَّلُ · يَتَفَاعَلُ

The exceptionForms V and VI are the only ones that keep fatḥa before the last letter: يَتَعَلَّمُ, يَتَنَازَعُ — not kasra.
يَـ prefixلَّ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
From the Qur’an
فَاقْصُصِ الْقَصَصَ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ
Form V, they (m.) — Sūrah al-A‘rāf, 7:176: “Relate the stories that perhaps they will reflect.”
لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ
Form V, they (m.) — Sūrah Ibrāhīm, 14:25: “that perhaps they will be reminded.”
فَيَتَعَلَّمُونَ مِنْهُمَا مَا يُفَرِّقُونَ بِهِ بَيْنَ الْمَرْءِ وَزَوْجِهِ
Form V, they (m.) — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:102: “and they learn from them what separates a man from his wife.”
وَالْمُطَلَّقَاتُ يَتَرَبَّصْنَ بِأَنفُسِهِنَّ ثَلَاثَةَ قُرُوءٍ
Form V, they (f.) — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:228: “Divorced women wait by themselves for three periods.”
إِذْ يَتَنَازَعُونَ بَيْنَهُمْ أَمْرَهُمْ
Form VI, they (m.) — Sūrah al-Kahf, 18:21: “when they disputed among themselves about their affair.”
يَتَعَارَفُونَ بَيْنَهُمْ
Form VI, they (m.) — Sūrah Yūnus, 10:45: “they will know one another.”
Forms VII · VIII

The hamzat-waṣl pair: يَنْفَعِلُ · يَفْتَعِلُ

The ruleThe opening ا disappears, the prefix takes fatḥa, and the vowel before the last letter becomes kasra.
يَـ prefixلِ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
!
Watch out for اسْتَمَعَ: it looks like Form X, but its س is a root letter (root س-م-ع), so it is Form VIII — يَسْتَمِعُ.
From the Qur’an
وَيَضِيقُ صَدْرِي وَلَا يَنطَلِقُ لِسَانِي
Form VII, he/it — Sūrah ash-Shu‘arā’, 26:13: “and my chest tightens and my tongue does not loosen.”
مِمَّن يَنقَلِبُ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيْهِ
Form VII, he — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:143: “from those who turn back on their heels.”
أَيَّ مُنقَلَبٍ يَنقَلِبُونَ
Form VII, they (m.) — Sūrah ash-Shu‘arā’, 26:227: “to what [final] turning they will turn.”
فَإِذَا هُمْ فَرِيقَانِ يَخْتَصِمُونَ
Form VIII, they (m.) — Sūrah an-Naml, 27:45: “and they became two groups disputing.”
وَالَّذِينَ يَجْتَنِبُونَ كَبَائِرَ الْإِثْمِ
Form VIII, they (m.) — Sūrah ash-Shūrā, 42:37: “and those who avoid the major sins.”
رَجُلَيْنِ يَقْتَتِلَانِ
Form VIII, they two — Sūrah al-Qaṣaṣ, 28:15: “two men fighting each other.”
فِيمَا كَانُوا فِيهِ يَخْتَلِفُونَ
Form VIII, they (m.) — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:113: “about that over which they used to differ.”
وَمِنْهُم مَّن يَسْتَمِعُ إِلَيْكَ
Form VIII (the tricky اسْتَمَعَ), he — Sūrah al-An‘ām, 6:25: “and among them are some who listen to you.”
Form IX

Colours and defects: يَفْعَلُّ

The ruleThe doubled last letter stays packed (shadda) before vowel endings — يَحْمَرُّ، يَحْمَرُّونَ — and unpacks before ـْنَ: يَحْمَرِرْنَ.
يَـ prefixمَ vowel before the doubled letterـُونَ ending
i
ابْيَضَّ and اسْوَدَّ have a weak letter in the root, but in Form IX it never changes — they conjugate like احْمَرَّ.
From the Qur’an
يَوْمَ تَبْيَضُّ وُجُوهٌ وَتَسْوَدُّ وُجُوهٌ
Form IX, she/it — Sūrah Āl ‘Imrān, 3:106: “On the Day some faces will turn white and some faces will turn black.” (وُجُوهٌ is a broken plural, so the verb is feminine singular.)
Form X

The “seeking” form: يَسْتَفْعِلُ

The ruleDrop the opening ا; keep سْتَ after the prefix; kasra before the last letter.
يَـ prefixفِ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
From the Qur’an
وَيَسْتَغْفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا
they (m.) — Sūrah Ghāfir, 40:7: “and they ask forgiveness for those who believe.”
وَالْمَلَائِكَةُ وَهُمْ لَا يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ
they (m.) — Sūrah an-Naḥl, 16:49: “and the angels, and they are not arrogant.”
وَيَسْتَعْجِلُونَكَ بِالْعَذَابِ
they (m.) + كَ — Sūrah al-Ḥajj, 22:47: “and they ask you to hasten the punishment.”
4-letter verbs · Part 1

الفِعْلُ الرُّبَاعِيّBare, and augmented by تَـ

The rules Bare فَعْلَلَ is a four-letter past → ḍamma prefix: يُوَسْوِسُ.
With تَـ تَفَعْلَلَ → fatḥa prefix, fatḥa before the last letter: يَتَدَحْرَجُ.
يُـ prefixوِ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
From the Qur’an — the bare pattern
الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ
he — Sūrah an-Nās, 114:5: “who whispers in the hearts of people.”
وَنَعْلَمُ مَا تُوَسْوِسُ بِهِ نَفْسُهُ
she/it — Sūrah Qāf, 50:16: “and We know what his soul whispers to him.”
i
I have no verse to show for the augmented pattern تَفَعْلَلَ.
4-letter verbs · Part 2

مَزِيدُ الرُّبَاعِيّ بِحَرْفَيْنِAugmented by two letters

The rules افْعَنْلَلَ → يَفْعَنْلِلُ (the ن stays, kasra before the last letter).
افْعَلَلَّ → يَفْعَلِلُّ — the doubled last letter packs before vowel endings and unpacks before ـْنَ: يَقْشَعْرِرْنَ.
يَـ prefixعِ vowel before the last letterـُونَ ending
From the Qur’an — the افْعَلَلَّ pattern
﴿تَقْشَعِرُّ مِنْهُ جُلُودُ الَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُمْ﴾
she/it — Sūrah az-Zumar, 39:23: “the skins of those who fear their Lord shiver at it.” (جُلُود is a broken plural, so the verb is feminine singular.)
أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
she/it — Sūrah ar-Ra‘d, 13:28: “Verily, in the remembrance of Allah hearts find rest.” The root has a hamza (ط-م-أ-ن), which changes its seat: يَطْمَئِنُّ.
i
I have no verse for افْعَنْلَلَ, and اطْمَأَنَّ is not in the conjugator because of its hamza.

أَحْسَنْتَ!
You can turn any augmented verb into the present tense

Drop the opening hamza, add the prefix, choose the prefix vowel by counting letters, and put kasra (or fatḥa for V and VI) before the last letter.
